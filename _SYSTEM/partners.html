FAQ

How do fees work?

GROUP STAY JAPAN redirects guests to an agreed booking channel. Listing and tracking terms are confirmed individually.

Is every application accepted?

No. We review legal compliance, operating quality, group suitability, content rights and inventory readiness before contracting.

What is needed before launch?

Property and room data, licensed photos, rates and availability, cancellation rules, house rules, legal business information, payout details and an executed agreement.
