GROUP STAY JAPAN is a multilingual accommodation discovery service focused on apartment hotels and spacious stays for families and groups of three or more traveling in Japan.

What we help travelers compare

We organize information such as maximum occupancy, room size, bed configuration, kitchens and shared living space. Travelers select their dates and group size, review the property details, and complete their reservation on a trusted booking partner website.

Booking and prices

GROUP STAY JAPAN does not currently process accommodation payments or issue booking confirmations. Live availability, final prices, taxes, payment, cancellation and booking support are provided by the booking partner selected by the traveler.

Affiliate disclosure

Some outbound links may be affiliate links. If a traveler completes a qualifying booking, GROUP STAY JAPAN may receive a referral commission. This does not add a separate fee to the traveler.
