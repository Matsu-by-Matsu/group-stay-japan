公式の原文
At our facility, guests staying for 7 nights or more will receive stay-over cleaning on the 4th day of their stay and every 7 days thereafter. For stays of 6 nights or fewer, stay-over cleaning is not provided. However, as part of our efforts to support the SDGs, we have adopted measures to reduce environmental impact. If you require towel or linen replacements, please do not hesitate to contact us.
