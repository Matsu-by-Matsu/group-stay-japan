Does everyone get a real bed?

A room for six does not always have six beds. We counted the beds in all 446 room types at the 78 apartment hotels we compare, using each hotel's official room details.

259 of the 446 room types (58%) give every guest a real bed at full capacity. 5 need checking on the official site.

How we counted

  • Single, semi-double and loft beds: 1 person each.
  • Double, queen and king beds: 2 people each.
  • Bunk beds: 2 people each.
  • Sofa beds, day beds and futons are counted separately, not as beds.

Source: each hotel's official room details, checked on 25 September 2026. Five room types describe their bunk beds in a way we could not count, so we left them out.

Where the rest of the group sleeps

Rooms for six

Of the 113 room types for six guests, 67 have real beds for all six. 37 have beds for four, 1 has beds for three, and 8 have beds for only two.

Before you book

  1. Check the bed types and counts, and whether sofa beds or futons are part of the capacity.
  2. Check how many bathrooms there are.
  3. For longer stays, check whether the washing machine is in the room or shared.
