What matters for six travelers

01 · Confirm one room

Verify that the selected room—not only the property—accommodates all six travelers.

02 · Match the sleeping layout

Capacity may include bunks, sofa beds or futons. Check the arrangement for adults and children.

03 · Think like two households

Separate sleeping zones, dining space, storage and bathroom access matter for two families or three generations.

04 · Prepare for several nights

A kitchen, refrigerator, dining table and laundry access reduce friction on longer city stays.

Compare the exact room before booking

A hotel’s maximum capacity does not apply to every room. These examples distinguish adult capacity, sleeping arrangements and laundry facilities. Room names below match the official English listings.

For six adults, do not book a four-adult room that allows two young children without beds. Compare the six-adult room types below. A bunk or loft bed requires climbing; choose a layout that suits everyone in your group.
