TOKYO MULTI-NIGHT STAYS

Tokyo Apartment Hotels with Kitchen and Laundry

A curated guide for families and groups staying three nights or longer. Every candidate has a kitchen; laundry is either inside the room or available on site, depending on the property and room type.

What makes a multi-night stay easier

01 · Confirm the selected room

Facilities can vary by room type. Reconfirm kitchen and laundry inside the room description.

02 · Check the full kitchen setup

Look for a cooktop, microwave, refrigerator, cookware, tableware and dining seats.

03 · Understand the laundry setup

Confirm washer-only versus washer-dryer and whether detergent is provided.

04 · Review stay services

Cleaning, towel changes, rubbish collection and restocking vary by property and plan.

Choose the laundry setup that fits your trip

In-room laundry is convenient for frequent small loads; on-site coin laundry works well when washing is planned around sightseeing.

A kitchen is more than a cost-saving feature

Breakfast, snacks and familiar meals become easier. Check cookware, tableware, refrigerator capacity and dining seats.

Think beyond the first night

For several nights, storage, rubbish collection, towel changes and cleaning frequency matter as much as bed count.

Frequently asked questions

Are these monthly apartments?

Not necessarily. This guide focuses on hotel-style stays that can be practical for several nights.

Does every listed property have an in-room washer?

No. Some list an in-room washer or washer-dryer; MIMARU provides either on-site coin laundry or in-room laundry depending on the property and room type.

Why are these properties included?

They are curated apartment hotels with group capacity, kitchens and a verified laundry option, rather than an unfiltered list of every Tokyo accommodation.
