What makes a multi-night stay easier

01 · Confirm the selected room

Facilities can vary by room type. Reconfirm kitchen and laundry inside the room description.

02 · Check the full kitchen setup

Look for a cooktop, microwave, refrigerator, cookware, tableware and dining seats.

03 · Understand the laundry setup

Confirm washer-only versus washer-dryer and whether detergent is provided.

04 · Review stay services

Cleaning, towel changes, rubbish collection and restocking vary by property and plan.

Frequently asked questions

Are these monthly apartments?

Not necessarily. This guide focuses on hotel-style stays that can be practical for several nights.

Does every listed property have laundry?

The current verified catalog explicitly lists a washer or washer-dryer, but confirm the selected room before booking.

Why is this list shorter?

We exclude properties when our current data does not explicitly verify both kitchen and laundry information.
