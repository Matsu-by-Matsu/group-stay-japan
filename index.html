Why an apartment hotel for your group?

Most hotel rooms in Japan are designed for one or two people, so a group of six often ends up booking three or more separate rooms. An apartment hotel keeps everyone in one unit, often with a kitchen and a washing machine. Divide the room price by the number of guests, and the cost per person can be lower than booking separate business hotel rooms.
